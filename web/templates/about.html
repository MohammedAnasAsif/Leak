How {{ site.name }} works

{{ site.description }}

  1. Listen. Every few minutes {{ site.name }} checks {{ source_total }} news feeds from every region of the world.
  2. Group. Reports about the same event are grouped into one story, so you see every outlet's take side by side instead of the same news ten times.
  3. Rank. Stories rise when many outlets in many regions cover them, and fall as they age. Nobody edits the order by hand.
  4. Link out. We show headlines and short summaries, credited, and always link to the original reporting.

Get {{ site.name }}

Install it straight from your browser (Add to Home Screen), or download the apps for Android and Windows from the latest release. iPhone users can sideload the iOS app; instructions are in the iOS guide.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cookies. Saved stories and settings stay on your device. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.
